## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

ShiftImport has evolved from a standalone smart PDF quadrant importer into a multi-tenant operational shift scheduling and collaboration platform. The platform features organizations, users, memberships, employees, areas, schedule versions, and change requests.

The purpose of **P5.7** is to rearchitect and unify the organizational model and user experience around a clear, intuitive, and robust conceptual framework:

$$\text{PERSONA} + \text{ACCESO} + \text{EMPLEO} + \text{ROL} + \text{SCOPE} + \text{ASIGNACIÓN OPERATIVA}$$

This model scales seamlessly from a small business (0 areas, 0 planners, Owner + Admin + 5 Employees) to a multi-area operational enterprise with specialized planners and hundreds of employees.

### Explicitly Out of Scope
To prevent unbounded scope creep, the following capabilities are strictly DEFERRED:
- Payroll (nóminas)
- Recruitment & hiring (contratación y selección)
- Performance appraisals & evaluations
- Complex HR document management & dossiers
- Clock-in & attendance tracking (fichaje completo R7)
- Generic approval workflow engine (R6)
- Shift Swap peer exchange (P5.6)
- Historical area reporting analytics (only active/current operational area is exposed in UI)

## 2. Product Principles & Invariants

1. **User and Employee are distinct entities**:
   - `User`: An authenticatable account with login credentials (`email`, `password_hash`, `session`).
   - `Employee`: An operational entity capable of being scheduled for shifts and appearing on rotas (`external_employee_id`, `name`).
   - Relationships:
     - `User` without `Employee`: E.g., external administrator or business owner.
     - `Employee` without `User`: Operational worker without app login.
     - `User` + `Employee`: Operational worker or manager with app access.
   - Invariant: Database tables `users` and `employees` MUST NOT be merged. The UI presents a unified "Persona" abstraction.
2. **Associated Employee does not diminish Role authority**:
   - An `ADMIN` or `OWNER` who also has an associated `Employee` maintains full administrative authority.
3. **Areas are strictly optional (0..N)**:
   - An organization can function with 0 areas. No system flow forces the creation of areas.
4. **Planners are strictly optional**:
   - An organization can operate with `OWNER`, `ADMIN`, and `EMPLOYEES` without any `PLANNER`.
5. **Flexible Planner Scopes**:
   - A planner can have an operational scope defined by:
     - `ORGANIZATION` (entire organization)
     - `AREAS` (one or more active areas)
     - `EMPLOYEES` (an explicit set of employees)
6. **Canonical Authorization Tuple**:
   - All authorization checks MUST evaluate:
     $$\text{can}(\text{actor}, \text{action}, \text{target})$$
7. **Effective-Dated Operational Assignments**:
   - Assignments between employees and areas, planners and areas, and planners and employees support temporal validity: `valid_from` and `valid_to`.
   - Historical assignments are never overwritten.
8. **Anti-Self-Approval**:
   - If a manager (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`, `PLANNER`) with an associated employee submits a request regarding their own shift, they CANNOT approve or reject their own request.
9. **Single Owner Invariant & Atomic Transfer**:
   - Exactly one `OWNER` per organization.
   - Ownership change cannot be performed via a routine role dropdown; it requires a dedicated `TRANSFER_OWNERSHIP` flow initiated by the active owner.
10. **Terminology**:
    - The navigation and workspace label for managers is **Solicitudes** / **Solicitudes pendientes** (never "Aprobaciones"), because requests may be either approved or rejected.

## 3. Navigation Information Architecture (Sidebar)

### 3.1 Structure by Role
The sidebar is capability-driven and eliminates obsolete, fragmented links ("Usuarios de la organización" and "Áreas" are superseded by "Equipo").

```
OPERACIÓN
- Calendario
- Planificar              (OWNER / ADMIN / PLANNER with planning capability)
- Importar turnos
- Añadir turno            (OWNER / ADMIN / PLANNER; or historical contribution for EMPLOYEE per contract)
- Solicitudes             (All authenticated roles: "Tus solicitudes" for Employee, "Solicitudes pendientes" for Manager)

GESTIÓN                   (OWNER / ADMIN, and read-only scope for authorized PLANNER)
- Equipo                  (Unified Personas, Roles & Access, Areas, Assignments)
- Historial de importaciones
- Formatos aprendidos

CONFIGURACIÓN
- Ajustes                 (OWNER / ADMIN)
```

### 3.2 EMPLOYEE Sidebar Contract
- **Operación**: Calendario, Importar turnos (Self), Añadir turno (historical contribution), Solicitudes ("Tus solicitudes").
- **Hidden**: Equipo, Áreas, Planificar, Historial de importaciones, Formatos aprendidos, Ajustes de organización.

## 4. Team Experience ("Equipo")

The primary management experience replaces fragmented modals with a unified, high-density workspace featuring 4 tabs:

### Tab 1: PERSONAS
- **Table Columns**:
  - Persona (Name, avatar/initials)
  - Email (or "Sin acceso")
  - Acceso (Acceso activo / Sin acceso)
  - Rol (Propietario / Admin / Planificador / Empleado / —)
  - Empleado (Vinculado con ID / Sin ficha de empleado)
  - Área actual (Área asignada o "Sin área")
  - Estado (Activo / Inactivo)
- **Actions**:
  - Search & filter by access, role, area, and status.
  - "Añadir persona" (Wizard: Identidad -> Acceso -> Empleo -> Scope -> Confirmación).
  - Row actions: Editar persona, Activar/Desactivar.

### Tab 2: ROLES Y ACCESO
- **Focus**: Authority, login access, role assignment, and planner scope.
- **Table Columns**: Persona, Rol, Ámbito / Scope, Login Email, Ficha Empleado, Estado.
- **Actions**:
  - Cambiar Rol (EMPLOYEE, PLANNER, ADMIN). Note: OWNER is NOT selectable in this dropdown.
  - Conceder acceso / Revocar acceso.
  - Gestionar Ámbito (para Planificadores: Organización / Áreas / Empleados).
  - "Transferir propiedad" (visible solely to current active OWNER).

### Tab 3: ÁREAS
- **When Organization has Areas**:
  - Cards or compact table listing: Nombre del Área, Código, Empleados activos, Planificadores asignados, Estado.
  - Actions: Nueva área, Editar, Gestionar empleados (bulk selector), Gestionar planificadores.
- **When Organization has 0 Areas**:
  - Informative empty state: "Esta organización no utiliza áreas."
  - CTA: "Crear área" (optional feature).

### Tab 4: ASIGNACIONES
- **Focus**: Operational relationships with bulk support.
- **Sub-views / Operations**:
  - Asignar Empleados a Área (Bulk move with effective date).
  - Asignar Áreas a Planificador (Multi-select checkboxes).
  - Asignar Empleados a Planificador (Searchable multi-select list with counter "X seleccionados").

## 5. Modal UX Contract (Desktop 1440×900)

1. **No Global Vertical Modal Scroll**:
   - Modals MUST fit within a standard 1440×900 desktop viewport.
   - No scrollbar extending across the entire modal from header to footer.
2. **Structure**:
   - Fixed header with modal title, subtitle, and close button.
   - Inner content area with controlled internal scrolling where necessary (e.g. employee lists, tables, pickers).
   - Fixed footer with primary action (Confirm/Save) and secondary action (Cancel/Back).

## 6. Formal Spec Changes (SPEC_CHANGE)

### SPEC_CHANGE 01: Planner Scope Modes
- **Old**: A PLANNER only has `memberships.scoped_area_id` (single area). If an org has active areas and `scoped_area_id` is null, the planner is completely blocked (`SCOPE_UNAVAILABLE`, PD D-05).
- **New**: A PLANNER can be assigned: (1) Entire Organization, (2) One or more Areas (`scoped_area_ids`), or (3) An explicit set of Employees (`scoped_employee_ids`).
- **Reason**: Multi-area operations and organizations without areas that delegate groups of employees to planners were unworkable.
- **Value**: High operational flexibility matching real-world airline/ground-handling and shift operations.
- **Compatibility Impact**: Non-breaking. Organizations with existing `memberships.scoped_area_id` are transparently backfilled/aliased.

### SPEC_CHANGE 02: Operational Assignment Temporal Validity
- **Old**: Employee area assignment was an in-place mutation of `employees.area_id`.
- **New**: Introduced effective-dated operational assignments table (`operational_assignments`) tracking `subject_type`, `subject_id`, `target_type`, `target_id`, `valid_from`, and `valid_to`.
- **Reason**: Moving an employee to a new area destroyed historical association for past shifts and imports.
- **Value**: Complete historical integrity without overwriting past rosters.
- **Compatibility Impact**: Non-breaking. `employees.area_id` is maintained as a cached snapshot of the current active assignment for legacy queries.

### SPEC_CHANGE 03: Ownership Transfer Protocol
- **Old**: An Admin or Owner could theoretically change role to Owner via `updateMemberRole`, which threw `OWNER_EXISTS` if an owner already existed.
- **New**: Dedicated transactional `/api/organizations/transfer-ownership` endpoint and modal. Only the current Owner can initiate it.
- **Reason**: Ownership transfer is a legal/organizational transfer of power, not an everyday role tweak.
- **Value**: Prevents accidental owner lockout or illicit privilege escalation.
- **Compatibility Impact**: Non-breaking. Existing memberships table and unique constraint are preserved.

### SPEC_CHANGE 04: Requests Navigation & Terminology
- **Old**: Sidebar navigation item labeled "Aprobaciones" pointing to `ApprovalInboxModal`.
- **New**: Navigation item labeled "Solicitudes" for both Employee and Manager. Opens "Tus solicitudes" for Employee and "Solicitudes pendientes" for Managers.
- **Reason**: Avoids bias ("Aprobaciones" implies every request is approved; requests can be rejected).
- **Value**: Professional, consistent, user-friendly language.
- **Compatibility Impact**: Frontend copy and route alignment; backend endpoints retain `/api/approval-requests` internally.

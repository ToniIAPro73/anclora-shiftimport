## 1. Executive Summary & Conceptual Shift

ShiftImport has evolved from an individual shift importer into an enterprise shift management platform with Organizations, Users, Memberships, Employees, Roles, Areas, Scheduling, Requests, and Approvals.

Phase 5.7 successfully unified and reorganized the entire platform around the canonical model:
```
PERSONA + ACCESO + EMPLEO + ROL + SCOPE + ASIGNACIÓN OPERATIVA
```

### Core Architecture Enforced:
1. **User != Employee Separation**:
   - `User` represents an authentication identity with login credentials.
   - `Employee` represents an operational person in shift rosters and scheduling.
   - The UI unifies them cleanly under the **Persona** abstraction without conflating database tables.
2. **Single Owner Guarantee & Atomic Ownership Transfer**:
   - Every organization has exactly 1 `OWNER`.
   - Ownership transfers atomically via dedicated endpoint `POST /api/organizations/transfer-ownership`.
3. **Planners with 3 Scope Modes**:
   - Areas are strictly optional (0..N).
   - Planners support:
     1. `ORGANIZATION`: full organization scope.
     2. `AREAS`: 1..N scoped areas with multi-select assignment.
     3. `EMPLOYEES`: explicit set of managed employees.
4. **Anti-Self-Approval Rule**:
   - Managers (Admin/Planner) linked to an Employee cannot approve or reject their own change requests.
   - Enforced server-side with 403 `self_approval_forbidden` on both approve and reject.
   - Enforced in UI with warning banner and disabled action buttons.
5. **Unified Terminology**:
   - "Solicitudes" / "Solicitudes pendientes" across navigation, inboxes, and tooltips (never "Aprobaciones").
6. **Effective-Dated Operational Assignments**:
   - Area assignments support historical tracking with `effective_date`.
   - Batch move operations via `/api/assignments/bulk-move`.
7. **Strict Desktop Viewport Contract (1440×900)**:
   - `ModalShell` with `workspace` mode ensures zero window scrollbar, fixed headers and footers, and internal container scrolling.
   - Zero double scrollbars across all modals.
   - Responsive down to 360px mobile view.

## 3. Canonical Business Scenarios Verification (A–G)

Verified via automated test suite `src/lib/scenarios-a-g.test.ts`:

- **Scenario A: Micro-empresa plana**:
  - 3 personas (1 Owner, 2 workers), 0 areas, 0 planners.
  - Roster builds cleanly; Owner has full governance; workers have no management access.
  - Zero areas empty state displays guidance without friction.
- **Scenario B: Empresa sin áreas con planificador**:
  - 1 Owner, 1 Planner with global scope, N employees.
  - Planner plans for any employee across the organization; cannot edit users or ownership.
- **Scenario C: Empresa con áreas y planificador acotado**:
  - 1 Owner, 2 areas (Norte, Sur), 1 Planner scoped strictly to Área Norte.
  - Planner can only edit shifts and approve requests within Área Norte; blocked from Área Sur and unassigned shifts.
- **Scenario D: Planificador multi-área**:
  - Planner assigned to multiple areas (Norte + Centro).
  - Operations permitted across both assigned areas; strictly forbidden in unassigned areas (Sur, Este).
- **Scenario E: Admin con empleo operativo**:
  - Admin linked to an operational Employee record.
  - Admin plans for peers; anti-self-approval strictly prevents approving/rejecting own change requests; permits deciding peer requests.
- **Scenario F: Owner sin empleo operativo**:
  - Owner with governance role only (`employeeId: null`, `isLinked: false`).
  - Does not appear in operational shift assignees; can manage users and transfer ownership.
- **Scenario G: Empleado con acceso**:
  - Employee portal user only.
  - Denied all management, area, and approver actions; navigation restricts access strictly to personal calendar and requests.
